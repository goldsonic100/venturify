Founder lessons, with permission

Learn from what didn’t work.

A post-mortem library should help founders make better decisions—not turn someone else’s hardest chapter into a spectacle. Stories belong here only when they’re real, shared with permission, and useful to other founders.

No verified founder post-mortems are published yet.

We’re building the consent and review process before publishing submissions. We won’t present fictional examples as real companies or imply a founder approved a story without their explicit permission.

The library is being prepared

We’ll publish stories after founders choose how they want to be identified, approve the final version, and can request edits or removal. Any figures will be shared only at the level of detail the founder agrees to.

What a useful post-mortem will include

The context and stage, the assumptions that didn’t hold, the signals the team noticed (or missed), what they would do differently, and practical lessons. Financial details will be optional and can use broad ranges. Private materials and identifying details about other people won’t be published without permission.
