Control who can discover you, who can message you, which Venturify features you use, and what notifications you receive. Changes save to your account.

Privacy & messaging

Public means discoverable to people on Venturify. Private profiles are visible to you and people you follow. Existing conversations remain available.

Public accountLet people view your founder profile and posts. Turn off to restrict your profile and posts to you and your existing followers.
Public DMsLet any founder start a direct conversation with you. When off, only founders who follow you can start a new DM with you; existing chats still work.
Allow collaboration requestsLet other founders send collaboration requests for ideas, resources, and projects.
Show me in DiscoverInclude your profile in founder discovery when you meet Venturify's Level 5+ and community-membership requirements.

Account features

Choose which publishing and opportunity tools you want enabled on your account.

StoriesAllow short-lived story updates on your profile and feed.
PollsAllow polls in posts and community discussions.
OpportunitiesAllow you to create and respond to idea, resource, and collaboration opportunities.

Notifications

Choose the activity you want Venturify to notify you about.

New messagesNotify you when someone sends you a direct or group message.
Message reactionsNotify you when someone reacts to one of your messages.
Group mentionsNotify you when someone mentions you in a group chat.

Appearance

Choose light or dark mode for every Venturify page. Your choice applies immediately and is saved to your account.

ThemeUse the system default or choose a light or dark appearance.
