    Built on trust

    Learn from failure. Don't exploit it.

    Founders should be able to share hard lessons without worrying that private details will be exposed or that an unverified claim will be presented as fact. Our goal is useful, respectful, well-labeled learning.

    Venturify is an early prototype. The public submission flow below is a demo and does not transmit or save entries. A secure review and storage system must be connected before accepting real confidential material.

    01

    Privacy before publication

    Remove identifying details when requested. Never publish sensitive personal data or confidential documents without permission.

    02

    Label the evidence

    Separate founder-reported figures, public records, estimates, and independently verified information.

    03

    Lessons over blame

    Focus on decisions, context, and takeaways — not humiliation, speculation, or hindsight pretending to be certainty.

    04

    Permission matters

    Pitch decks and financial materials may be confidential or copyrighted. Share only material you have the right to disclose.
