Venturify

Built for honest founder learning.

Venturify exists to help founders learn from real operating decisions, not polished startup myth. We collect lessons from the messy middle of building: the experiments, the mistakes, the numbers, and the tradeoffs that matter most.

Open the feed Read the mission
Goal

Make startup knowledge more useful and more real.

The goal of Venturify is simple: help founders make better decisions by learning from the actual realities of building a business.

  • Turn founder lessons into practical knowledge that is usable on the next decision.
  • Make operating context visible: what changed, why it changed, and what it cost.
  • Reduce startup learning gaps by capturing honest failures, pivots, and tradeoffs.
  • Create a safer place for learning where context matters more than ego.
What we do not do

We do not reward hype.

We are not a place for vague startup inspiration, fake success stories, or polished PR designed to impress strangers.

  • No fake company stories or invented traction.
  • No anonymous claims without enough context to verify.
  • No simplistic “founder myth” posts that hide real tradeoffs.
  • No moralizing around failure without acknowledging uncertainty and constraints.
Principles

What we believe

1

Be honest about uncertainty

Founders are making decisions under incomplete information. Good lessons explain the assumptions, the bets, and what was still unknown.

2

Share numbers, not just stories

We value concrete operating detail: CAC, burn, retention, hiring, churn, bottlenecks, and what changed when the model broke.

3

Learn from failure without glorifying it

Failure is not a badge. It is a signal. We treat mistakes as useful data when they are described with enough context to help others avoid the same trap.

4

Respect founder context

Every decision happens inside a specific market, team, and moment. We avoid content that ignores context or reduces complex decisions to simplistic advice.

5

Give credit and protect consent

People should be able to share lessons without being exposed, misrepresented, or pressured into posting details they do not want to share publicly.

6

Keep the community useful

Venturify is not a vanity feed. We prioritize substantive lessons, useful systems, and respectful discussion over noise, ego, and hype.

How it works

Good founder posts have real context

1

State the decision

Tell the reader what changed, what the team was trying to solve, and why the decision mattered.

2

Add the operating details

Include the actual numbers, constraints, timing, and tradeoffs so others can understand the full picture.

3

Share the lesson

Explain what you learned, what you would do differently, and what remains uncertain.

Rules for the community

What makes a contribution valuable

  • Describe the problem clearly, not just the outcome.
  • Include process and context, not just a moral lesson.
  • Be specific about what worked, what failed, and why.
  • Keep critiques constructive and truthful.
  • Protect people and privacy: do not shame, leak, or fabricate.
